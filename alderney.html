Alderney

The island

A relaxing holiday, or a holiday packed with adventures

From stunning views to pleasant walks, Alderney has a wonderful selection of nature and activities. With a slow pace of life, it’s perfect for a relaxing holiday or an escape from the bursting life of a city, with a very visual historical past that spans many centuries.

Alderney’s beaches are boasted as the best in the Channel Islands, with crystal clear waters and soft sand. Whether you want to unwind or fill your days with adventures, Alderney has it all.

Learn more from Visit Alderney

Things to do

Everything worth doing, a short stroll away

From our front door on Victoria Street, the whole island opens up. Here’s where to start.

Nature

Beaches & bays

Stroll down Braye Road to some of the finest beaches in the Channel Islands, with crystal clear water and soft sand.

Explore

Bike to the lighthouse

Take a bike out to the lighthouse, or wander out to Gannet Rock to watch the birds wheel over the sea.

Wildlife

Wildlife centre

The Alderney Wildlife centre is minutes from the hotel — a lovely introduction to the island’s nature.

History

Alderney museum

Discover centuries of island history, all a few minutes’ walk from La Ville.

Wander

Cobbled Victoria Street

Shops, restaurants and the beautiful St Anne’s Church — the Cathedral of the Channel Islands — right outside.

Slow down

Do nothing at all

Perch on a bench with a good book, or simply work on your tan. Alderney is made for it.
