The best start

Breakfast, every morning

The most important meal of the day for adventuring and exploring around Alderney. Breakfast is served between 7:30am and 10:00am daily, and it’s included with every room.

We use the best, locally sourced ingredients wherever possible — let us know about any dietary requirements when you book and we’ll look after you.
