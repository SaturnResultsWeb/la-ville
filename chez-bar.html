The Chez Bar

The island’s liveliest bar

The largest bar on Alderney

Right next door to the hotel, the Chez Bar is where the island comes to celebrate. With regular DJs and live music, we serve the best selection of rum, gin, draft lager and real ales.

With two 50” screens showing Sky TV and TNT Sports, it’s the only place to be to watch all the big sporting events. The perfect venue for a party, a match, or a proper night out.
