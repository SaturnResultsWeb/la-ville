Welcome to La Ville

The heart of island life, on Victoria Street

With views of St Anne’s Church, one of the finest Victorian buildings in the Channel Islands, and the beautiful cobbled Victoria Street, La Ville is perfectly placed for any Alderney excursion.

Everything here is built around great food, good drinks, comfortable rooms and the best service on the island. Whether you’re working on your tan, exploring somewhere new, or simply escaping, La Ville will be the place for you.

Explore Alderney

Everything worth doing is a short stroll away

The Wildlife centre, the museum, shops and restaurants are all a few minutes from our front door. Take a bike out to the lighthouse, wander to Gannet Rock, stroll down Braye Road to one of the finest beaches in the Channel Islands, or just perch on a bench with a good book.
